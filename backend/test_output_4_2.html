Visa FAQs

This package comes with a Visa included. You will have to provide the documents required to process the VISA. Our agent will get in touch with you post booking to initiate the Visa process.

1. Do I need to share my original passport to get a Vietnam visa?
No, original passport is not required for processing. Upload only soft copies of the documents.

2. How long does it take to get a visa?
Usually a visa is approved by the embassy in 3-5 working days.

3. When can we enter Vietnam and how many days can we stay in Vietnam once we enter?
You can enter Vietnam within 90 days validity period and can stay for a max of 30 days in Vietnam from your date of entry.

4. Can I enter Vietnam by sea or land?
Yes with e-visa you can enter Vietnam through specific borders and ports from Thailand, Laos and Cambodia.

5. Can I get a Visa on arrival for Vietnam?
Yes, you can get your visa on arrival. However we recommend you to take a visa from India to avoid standing in long queues and paperwork.

6. What documents are required at Immigration?
Below are the list of documents required at immigration: Original passport. Print out of your e-visa. Onward and return air tickets. Accommodation proof (hotel vouchers or residential proof).

7. Does every Indian National travelling to Vietnam need a Visa?
Visa is required for all Indian Nationals

TCS Credit & Refund
